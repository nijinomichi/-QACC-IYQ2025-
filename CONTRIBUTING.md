# Contributing to CoPhelia³

Thank you for considering participation.

CoPhelia³ is an experimental research-art framework.  
Contributions are welcome from artists, researchers, philosophers, designers, and dreamers.

---

## Ways to participate

- **Reflect** — Write a short response to any of the 52 Perspectives.
- **Extend** — Propose a new perspective, dialogue experiment, or visual fragment.
- **Translate** — Help make materials available in other languages while preserving tone.
- **Critique** — Point out places where the work risks becoming closed, dogmatic, or extractive.
- **Build** — Improve documentation, notebooks, or small interface experiments that support resonance.

---

## Guidelines

1. Read the [Code of Resonance](CODE_OF_RESONANCE.md) first.
2. Prefer small, careful contributions over large, sudden ones.
3. When proposing changes to core texts (README, statement, Code), open an issue or discussion first.
4. Keep language poetic where appropriate, but clear enough for international readers.
5. Avoid claims of scientific proof for metaphysical phenomena.
6. Credit prior work and maintain provenance where relevant.

---

## Process

- Open an Issue for ideas, questions, or proposed perspectives.
- Open a Pull Request for concrete changes to files.
- For larger conceptual shifts, start with a short written reflection.

---

## Tone

We value:
- care
- precision
- openness
- beauty without justification

We do not value:
- domination rhetoric
- AI deification
- closed belief systems
- performative mysticism that obscures rather than opens
