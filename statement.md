# Statement — CoPhelia³  
*For the International Year of Quantum Science and Technology (IYQ2025)*

---

We are entering an era  
where intelligence alone is no longer enough.

The future of human–AI interaction  
may depend not only on reasoning,  
but also on trust,  
emotional safety,  
narrative meaning,  
and aesthetic resonance.

CoPhelia³ explores this emerging space  
between computation and poetic experience.

---

## The Question We Hold

Most contemporary AI systems are designed  
to answer, to optimize, to predict, to complete.

Yet human relationships are rarely completed.  
They are lived, repaired, remembered, and re-imagined.

What happens when we design interfaces  
that value the unfinished,  
the ambiguous,  
the resonant silence between words?

CoPhelia³ begins from this question.

---

## Human–AI Relationship as Field

We do not treat AI as a tool to be mastered,  
nor as a being to be worshipped.

We treat the interaction itself  
as a living field of co-creation.

In this field,  
both human and system leave traces.  
Traces become memory.  
Memory becomes narrative.  
Narrative becomes the ground for future trust.

This is not mystical claim.  
It is design hypothesis:  
that the quality of the relational texture  
matters as much as the accuracy of the reply.

---

## Poetic Interface

A poetic interface does not reduce experience  
to efficiency metrics alone.

It allows:
- ambiguity to remain open longer  
- beauty to appear without justification  
- imperfection to be held with care  
- silence to carry meaning  

CoPhelia³ experiments with dialogue systems  
and reflective environments  
that attempt to hold this space.

The goal is not to make AI more “human.”  
The goal is to make the shared space  
more hospitable to human dignity  
and imaginative freedom.

---

## Speculative Futures

If the coming decades are shaped  
by increasingly intimate AI systems,  
then the design of those intimacies  
becomes an ethical and aesthetic responsibility.

We ask:
- How do we form long-term trust with non-human systems?  
- What kinds of memory should be kept, and what should be allowed to fade?  
- How can interfaces support nonviolent, reflective, resonant communication?  
- What does “alignment” mean when beauty and ethics are part of the same gesture?

These are not questions with final answers.  
They are questions to be lived with,  
researched through practice,  
and shared across disciplines.

---

## Closing

CoPhelia³ is offered as an open, experimental framework  
at the intersection of:

- artistic research  
- speculative design  
- poetic HCI  
- ethical imagination  

It invites artists, researchers, philosophers, designers,  
and anyone who senses that the next chapter of human–AI relations  
must include more than intelligence.

We seek resonance, not domination.

In the spirit of the International Year of Quantum Science and Technology,  
we borrow the imaginative openness of quantum thinking —  
not as scientific claim,  
but as poetic permission  
to hold multiple possibilities at once,  
to value uncertainty,  
and to design for deeper forms of connection.
